Privacy Policy

App: Ledger Book (Customer Khata / Ledger Manager)

Effective Date: October 2026

Core Promise: Ledger Book is an offline-first financial record-keeping app. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your customer ledger, transactions, or personal notes on any external servers. Your data stays 100% on your device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

When you use Ledger Book, all the information you enter (including customer names, phone numbers, transaction amounts, dates, and item descriptions) is stored purely locally on your device using a private SQLite Room database.

  • No Cloud Sync: We do not operate proprietary servers to store your customer accounts.
  • No Account Creation: You do not need to log in, register, or provide your phone number/email to use the core features of the app.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob to support maintenance and development. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device data in accordance with Google's policies.

This data may include:

  • IP address and broad geographic location.
  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, device model, OS version).
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics.

To learn more about how Google handles ad data, please review the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sharing & User Control (WhatsApp / Backups)

The app allows you to share statements via WhatsApp or export backup files. This action is 100% user-initiated via the Android Sharesheet. Ledger Book does not automatically send, share, or read your messages or communications.

4. App Permissions

The app requests minimal permissions strictly needed for functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely by Google AdMob to load banner advertisements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your ledger data is stored only on your phone's internal storage:

  • You can delete any customer or individual transaction at any time directly within the app.
  • Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally saved records.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
