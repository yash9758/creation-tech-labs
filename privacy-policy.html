Privacy Policy

Application: Ledger Book (Customer Khata / Ledger Manager)

Effective Date: October 2026

Core Commitment: Ledger Book is an offline-first financial record-keeping utility. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your customer names, balances, or transactions on external servers. All personal records remain stored exclusively on your device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

When you use Ledger Book, all data you enter (customer names, phone numbers, transaction amounts, timestamps, and item notes) is stored strictly in your phone's internal storage via an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite Room database.

  • No Cloud Storage: We do not operate remote servers to back up or read your financial entries.
  • No Account Registration: No account signup, email, or identity verification is required.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app serves advertisements through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, OS build, device model).
  • Broad location data (country/city level via IP).
  • Ad performance metrics and interactions.

For more information on Google's practices, please refer to the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

3. User-Initiated Data Sharing

Ledger statements and export files shared via WhatsApp or local backup are triggered entirely by explicit user action using the standard Android Sharesheet. The application does not automatically scan, transmit, or monitor your communications.

4. Application Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to fetch banner ads.

5. Data Deletion and Retention

You have complete control over your records:

  • Individual entries or complete customer accounts can be deleted permanently within the app.
  • Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all saved database records.
